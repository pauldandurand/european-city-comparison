Sunny days: one consistent climate method

* A sunny day receives at least 60% of possible daylight as sunshine. Counts are calculated from Open-Meteo's ERA5 reanalysis using daily sunshine duration and daylight duration for 1991–2020, sampled at each city's central coordinates, then averaged per year and rounded. This uses the same definition for every city; it does not use the earlier incompatible weather estimates.

The 30-year daily series is loaded from Open-Meteo when the page opens online; values appear as they are computed. Until fetched, a dash is shown. If the service is unreachable, values remain unavailable rather than invented. ERA5 gridded model data are estimates, not observations at identical weather stations.

Weather dataset and API documentation. Sunshine is defined by direct normal irradiance above 120 W/m².

Housing prices: municipal market benchmarks

Rent (€/m²/month) is an indicative monthly residential rental price; Home price (€/m²) is an indicative sale-price benchmark. France uses November 2026 MeilleursAgents apartment market estimates. Spain, Portugal and Italy use Idealista residential listing indices from September 2026. Figures are not necessarily medians of identical 80 m² two-bedroom homes; property types, asking versus estimated prices, and provider methods differ.

Municipality boundaries

Population and density refer to the city proper (municipality), not the surrounding metropolitan area. Density divides the municipality's 2023 population by its entire administrative land area, including green space and undeveloped land.

Cross-country data caution

Poverty and higher-education measures are from differing national sources, age groups and years. French poverty is individual disposable-income based, Italy's comparison uses households, and Portugal's is based on declared gross income. The default poverty sort is numerical, not a harmonized European ranking.
